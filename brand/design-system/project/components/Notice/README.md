Notice is a short message in the flow: a tip, the one thing that needs a look, or the reason something can't be saved.

- `info` (`tinta-soft`): a calm tip. "You can change the times later."
- `look` (`highlighter` with `on-highlighter`): the single most important thing on the screen. At most one per screen.
- `stop` (`stop-soft`, `stop` icon): why the user can't go on yet, and what to do about it.

The consumer provides an optional bold `title` and one or two sentences. Say what to do next; never apologise, never say "error".
