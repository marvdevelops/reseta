DoseCard is what opens when an alarm rings: whose medicine, when, what and how much, the pill photo, and three plain choices.

The consumer provides `person` as the family says it ("Lola Rosa"), `time`, `medicine` with strength, `amount`, an optional `instruction` ("after breakfast"), the pill `photo` the user took, and the four handlers.

- "I took it" is the only primary button. "Remind me in 10 minutes" is secondary; "Skip this dose" and "Read aloud" are quiet.
- The person's name comes first and biggest: caregivers manage several people on one phone.
- Times and amounts are in the mono face so 1 and 7, 0 and 8 can't be confused.
